Jaddid - Recyclable Materials & Eco-Friendly Products Marketplace Guide

=== ABOUT JADDID ===
Jaddid is an innovative marketplace connecting sellers and buyers of recyclable materials and eco-friendly products. Our platform promotes sustainability by facilitating the trade of raw recyclable materials and finished products made from recycled content.

=== MATERIAL CATEGORIES ===

1. PLASTIC
   - PET bottles and containers
   - HDPE (milk jugs, detergent bottles)
   - PVC pipes and fittings
   - LDPE bags and films
   - PP (yogurt containers, bottle caps)
   - PS (foam packaging, disposable cups)
   Common uses: New bottles, clothing fibers, construction materials

2. PAPER & CARDBOARD
   - Newspapers and magazines
   - Office paper (white, colored)
   - Cardboard boxes
   - Paper packaging
   Common uses: New paper products, cardboard, insulation

3. METAL
   - Aluminum cans and foil
   - Copper wiring and pipes
   - Steel cans and scrap
   - Iron and mixed metals
   - Brass fittings
   Common uses: New metal products, construction materials

4. GLASS
   - Clear glass bottles and jars
   - Colored glass (green, brown, blue)
   - Broken glass cullet
   Common uses: New glass containers, fiberglass, construction

5. WOOD
   - Wooden pallets
   - Sawdust and wood chips
   - Scrap lumber
   - Wood shavings
   Common uses: Particle board, mulch, biofuel, compost

6. ELECTRONICS (E-Waste)
   - Circuit boards
   - Computer components
   - Mobile phones
   - Cables and wires
   - Batteries (handled separately)
   Common uses: Metal recovery, refurbishment

7. TEXTILES
   - Old clothing
   - Fabric scraps
   - Curtains and linens
   Common uses: Rags, insulation, new fabrics

8. ORGANIC WASTE
   - Food scraps
   - Yard waste
   - Agricultural residues
   Common uses: Compost, biofuel, animal feed

=== PRICING GUIDELINES ===

Typical Price Ranges (EGP per kg):
- Plastic (PET): 3-8 EGP/kg
- Paper/Cardboard: 1-4 EGP/kg
- Aluminum: 15-25 EGP/kg
- Copper: 60-100 EGP/kg
- Steel: 2-6 EGP/kg
- Glass: 0.5-2 EGP/kg
- Wood chips: 1-3 EGP/kg
- Textiles: 2-10 EGP/kg

Prices vary based on:
- Material quality and cleanliness
- Quantity (bulk discounts available)
- Market demand
- Processing required
- Location and transport costs

=== MATERIAL CONDITIONS ===

EXCELLENT: Clean, sorted, ready for processing
GOOD: Minimal contamination, mostly sorted
ACCEPTABLE: Some mixed materials, needs sorting
POOR: High contamination, requires significant processing

=== QUALITY REQUIREMENTS ===

To ensure materials can be recycled:
- Remove labels and caps when possible
- Clean containers (rinse food residue)
- Separate different material types
- Keep materials dry
- Avoid contamination with hazardous materials

=== SUSTAINABILITY TIPS ===

1. Reduce before recycling - minimize waste at the source
2. Proper sorting increases material value
3. Clean materials fetch better prices
4. Bulk quantities preferred by industrial buyers
5. Local transactions reduce carbon footprint
6. Partner with collection centers for steady supply

=== COMMON QUESTIONS ===

Q: What materials have the highest value?
A: Metals (especially copper and aluminum) and clean electronics typically have the highest recycling value.

Q: How much can I earn from recycling?
A: Depends on material type, quantity, and quality. Industrial quantities generate significant income.

Q: Do you accept mixed materials?
A: Sorted materials are preferred and fetch better prices, but mixed materials can be listed with proper disclosure.

Q: How is delivery arranged?
A: Sellers and buyers negotiate delivery. Platform supports both pickup and delivery options.

Q: Are there minimum quantities?
A: No minimum for listings, but bulk quantities are more attractive to industrial buyers.
